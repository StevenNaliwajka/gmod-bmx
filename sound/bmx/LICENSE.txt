sound/bmx/ -- the addon's own sounds

bell1.wav .. bell4.wav          a small lever bicycle bell, "ting-ting"
horn1.wav horn2.wav             an electric motorbike horn
tick1.wav .. tick3.wav          a freewheel pawl over one ratchet tooth
engine_4t.wav                   a four-stroke single (the dirt bike), loop
engine_2t.wav                   a 50 cc two-stroke (the moped), loop
motor.wav                       an electric hub motor's whine, loop
roll_tyre.wav                   bicycle tyres on tarmac, loop
roll_wheel.wav                  urethane wheels on concrete, loop

These are not recordings and contain no sampled material. Each file is
computed from first principles (the partials of a struck steel dome; a buzzing
diaphragm through a horn's resonance; pressure pulses through an exhaust's
resonances; shaped noise) by tools/sound/make_sounds.py in this repository,
which regenerates them byte for byte. The bell's partial frequencies, decay
times and strike timing are numbers measured off a reference recording the
owner chose; no audio from it is used. They are original work and are released
under the same MIT licence as the code (see LICENSE at the root of the
repository). You may use them for anything, with or without credit.
