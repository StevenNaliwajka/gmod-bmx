sound/bmx/ -- the addon's own sounds

bell1.wav bell2.wav bell3.wav   a bicycle bell, "ring-ring"
horn1.wav horn2.wav             an electric motorbike horn

These are not recordings and contain no sampled material. Each file is
computed from first principles (the partials of a struck steel dome; a buzzing
diaphragm through a horn's resonance) by tools/sound/make_sounds.py in this
repository, which regenerates them byte for byte. They are original work and
are released under the same MIT licence as the code (see LICENSE at the root
of the repository). You may use them for anything, with or without credit.
